# Follow-up Gameplay Research Notes — 2026-08-22

## Official store evidence

### Google Play

Source: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.babuyo.footy.tc.android&hl=en_US

The public listing describes two named modes: Player mode and Coach mode. Player mode starts the user as a 15-year-old talented teenager joining a professional club, with a 20-year career involving competitions, training, transfers, football skills, and championship pursuit. Coach mode starts as a retired star and emphasizes dealing with big clubs, developing the mother club, changing formations, and flexible tactics. The listing does not expose the full Versus ruleset in its descriptive text.

### Apple App Store

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/football-rising-star/id1585604439

The public listing repeats the Player/Coach dual-mode description, fast simulation rhythm, diverse tactics, trophies, and achievements. The visible current “What’s New” text says “Fixed several bugs.” The listing is useful for official Player/Coach claims and high-level product framing, but it does not provide complete numeric formulas or the internal Versus settlement rules.

## Research boundary

Store listings are official public evidence for named modes and high-level mechanics. They are not sufficient evidence for exact server formulas, auction costs, Scout effects, Sponsor payout/cooldown, matchmaking, or authoritative Versus tables. Versus-specific screen evidence remains anchored to the dedicated walkthrough `V8MsDUXNl8A` and the Versus segment in review `KQiUcv9d25c`.
