The visible gameplay of Football Rising Star Coach Mode follows a structured cycle of management, events, and match simulations.

### **Season Progression & Management**
*   **Club Selection & Job Offers:** Initial club selection occurs at **00:02**. Subsequent job offers and club changes are visible at **07:58**, **25:20**, **25:34**, and **25:40** (joining Livorno). A final job offer screen appears at **55:04** after a season ends.
*   **Board Meetings & Targets:** Seasonal goals are set during meetings: reaching the A-League (**05:37**) and avoiding relegation (**08:19**, **25:47**).
*   **Transfer Market:** The market is accessed through several tabs:
    *   **List:** Browsing available players (**00:11**, **01:04**, **08:26**, **20:02**, **40:03**, **41:15**, **42:23**).
    *   **Scout:** Hiring and setting parameters for scouts (**01:10**, **01:48**, **02:22**, **05:06**, **51:18**, **52:30**).

    *   **Deal:** Finalizing transfers and signings (**03:54**, **05:11**, **11:15**, **51:15**, **52:27**).
*   **Formation & Tactics:** Set before each match simulation (**06:10**, **08:48**), with adjustments possible during halftime (**07:01**, **09:27**).

*   **Awards:** Trophies and ceremonies are shown for Coach of the Year (**00:44**), general Award Ceremonies (**05:15**), and Best of the Round (**25:07**).
*   **Resource Gates:** Energy recovery via video ads is visible at **24:09**.

### **Random Choice Events**
The game features various interactive events that require a choice from the coach:
*   **Team-Building:** "Go Fishing" (**00:41**), "Island Tour" (**00:49**), and "Tea Time" (**22:16**).
*   **Press & Media:** "The Leak of Tactics" (**00:54**) and "TV Show" invitations (**22:58**, **25:30**).
*   **Player & Locker Room Management:**
    *   **Locker Room Speeches:** Encouraging or addressing the team (**11:43**, **14:43**, **15:44**, **19:55**, **21:04**, **23:31**, **31:04**, **42:16**).
    *   **Discipline & Cliquing:** Handling player groups ("Clique" at **05:19**) and "Locker room crisis" events (**10:04**, **11:04**, **13:36**, **17:37**, **28:30**, **36:54**, **38:30**).
*   **Training & Youth:** "Youth Training" sessions (**07:37**) and evaluating a "Youth System Star" (**19:47**).
*   **Financial:** "The Budget" event where the coach can strive for more funds (**47:53**).
*   **Club Interaction:** Responding to "Team Doctors" (**24:50**) and dealing with the fallout of failing seasonal goals ("Fail to reach" at **54:46**).
