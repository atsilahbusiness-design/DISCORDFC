Based on the visual and audio analysis of the video, here is a detailed breakdown of the observable user experience (UX) and navigation flow for **Football Rising Star**.

### **1. Main Menu and Mode Selection**
The main menu (00:04) features a vertical layout with a central character and three primary game modes.

*   **Visible Mode Labels:**
    *   **Mode Pemain** (Player Mode) - Marked with a "HOT" tag.
    *   **Mode Pelatih** (Coach Mode)
    *   **Vs Versus Mode**
*   **Top Right Icons:**
    *   **Pencapaian** (Achievements)
    *   **Pengaturan** (Settings)
*   **Versus Mode Observation:** While a button labeled **"Vs Versus Mode"** is clearly visible on the main menu (00:04, 01:13), the video **does not show any gameplay, lobbies, or screens associated with this mode.** The entire demonstration focuses on "Mode Pemain."

### **2. Navigation Flow: Player Mode (Mode Pemain)**
The user navigates through a career simulation path: Role Creation $\rightarrow$ Profile Home $\rightarrow$ Training/Advancing Weeks $\rightarrow$ Match Simulation $\rightarrow$ Rewards/Upgrades.

#### **A. Role Creation (01:39 - 02:22)**
*   **Labels:** "Buat peran" (Create role).
*   **Birthplace Selection:** China, Argentina, Brazil, England, Spain, Italy, Germany, France, America, Others (a sub-menu for specific countries like Indonesia is shown at 02:05).
*   **Avatar Selection:** Four distinct illustrated character portraits.
*   **Position Selection:** ST, CF, LW, RW, CAM, LM, RM, CM, CDM, LB, RB, CB, GK.
*   **Nickname Input:** A text field at the bottom.

#### **B. Player Profile / Home Screen (02:23)**
*   **Stats Radar/Bar:** PHY (Physical), OFF (Offense), SPD (Speed), DEF (Defense), STA (Stamina), TEC (Technique).
*   **Navigation Buttons:**
    *   **Latihan** (Training)
    *   **Ronde Berikutnya** (Next Round/Week)
    *   **Ranking**
    *   **Pencapaian** (Achievements)

#### **C. Training & Skill Setup (03:19, 03:50)**
*   **Gunakan EXP (Use EXP):** A vertical list of attributes with "+" buttons to upgrade:
    *   Tembakan (Shooting), Penalti (Penalty), Sundulan (Heading), Operan (Passing), Dribel (Dribbling), Tendangan bebas (Free Kick), Lari tanpa bola (Off-ball run), Menahan pemain bertahan (Defending), Kerja sama tim (Teamwork), Daya tahan (Stamina), Kecepatan (Speed), Tekad (Will).
*   **Pelatihan (Training Menu):**
    *   **Routine:** Shooting, Passing, Dribbling, etc.
    *   **Trick Training:** Specific moves like "Tendangan Salto" (Bicycle Kick) or "Operan Tumit" (Heel Pass).
    *   **Private Training:** Categorized as Junior, Senior, or Expert.

#### **D. Match Simulation & Results (03:08, 06:17, 10:23)**
*   **Match Interface:** Matches are simulated textually. A screen shows the two team names (e.g., Sochaux vs. Triumphal) and the final score.
*   **Lineup/Ratings Screen:** A table listing players by position (GK, CB, LB, CDM, etc.) alongside their match ratings (e.g., 7.5, 8.2).
*   **Match Rewards:** "Gunakan EXP" button appears after matches to distribute earned experience points.

#### **E. Standings & League Screens (03:42, 05:02, 08:24)**
*   **Tabel Liga (League Table):** A standard league table showing:
    *   Rank, Team Name, Matches Played (Main), Wins (M), Draws (S), Losses (K), Goal Difference (Selisih Gol), and Points (Poin).
*   **Contract Screen (04:23, 06:55):** Shows current club, league, salary, and options for "Ajukan" (Apply), "Transfer," and "Negosiasi" (Negotiation).

#### **F. Reward & Trophy Screens**
*   **Man of the Match (06:49):** A popup titled "Pemain terbaik dari pertandingan" with a gold trophy icon.
*   **Aula (Trophy Room) (07:10):** A gallery of potential awards:
    *   **Personal:** Player of the Year, UEFA Player of the Year, European Golden Shoe.
    *   **League Specific:** Best Player in English, Spanish, Italian, German, and French leagues.

### **3. General UX Observations**
*   **Orientation:** Vertical (Portrait) mode only.
*   **Interaction Style:** Heavily menu-based and "clicker" style; there is no manual control of the player during matches.
*   **Events:** Random event popups occur, such as "Sundulan dua poin" (08:29), which provide attribute bonuses based on user choices.
