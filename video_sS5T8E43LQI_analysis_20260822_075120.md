This analysis details the user experience (UX) and screen flow observed in the *Football Rising Star* walkthrough, focusing on the interface elements and navigation paths.

### **1. Navigation and Main Hub**
The game utilizes a persistent bottom navigation bar for primary mode switching:
*   **Player (Home):** Displays the current date, player status (e.g., "Free Player" or current club), basic attributes (PHY, SPD, DEF, STA, TEC), and a news feed/log of recent events.
*   **Training:** Accesses skill development and attribute upgrades.
*   **Match (Central Ball Icon):** Initiates the next scheduled match or advances the week.
*   **Achievements:** Tracks milestones like "Here We Go" (starting lineup) and "My First MVP."
*   **System/Settings:** Likely for game options, though not heavily explored.

### **2. Player Management and Progression**
Progression is divided between active training and post-match attribute allocation:
*   **Regular Training:** A menu for upgrading core stats (Shots, Pass, Dribbling, etc.) using currency.
*   **Trick Training:** A skill tree where specific moves (e.g., Bicycle Kick, Scorpion Kick) are unlocked once attribute thresholds are met.
*   **Personal Training:** Allows hiring specialized trainers (Junior, Senior, Expert) to boost training efficiency.
*   **Experience Distribution:** Following a match, an "Assign Exp" screen appears. Players manually distribute earned points across twelve specific attributes via a slider/plus-button interface.

### **3. Club and Career Management**
The career path is managed through specific UI prompts:
*   **Signing:** Success screens appear when joining a new club (e.g., Triumphal Arch, Sandhausen), showing the club crest and a welcome message.
*   **Personal/Contract Screen:** Displays financial status (Money, Income, Spending), current contract details (Salary), and options for "Ask for pay," "Transfer," and "Negotiation."
*   **Negotiation UI:** A pop-up window displays club interest and allows the player to "View," "Reject," or "Join" based on league rank and offered salary.

### **4. Match Flow and Simulation**
The match experience follows a standardized sequence:
*   **Pre-Match/Lineup:** A screen showing the starting XI for both teams with individual player ratings. The user’s player is highlighted (e.g., ST).
*   **Match Simulation:** The match occurs off-screen, transitioning directly to the result.
*   **Result Screen:** Shows the final score, total audience, and a detailed list of player ratings for both sides.
*   **League Results & Standings:** A summary of all round results is shown, followed by the "League Tables" displaying standard metrics (Played, Won, Drawn, Lost, GD, Points).

### **5. Tactics and Formation**
While the user does not manually drag players to change formations in this walkthrough, tactical elements are visible:
*   **Lineup UI:** Displays players in standard positions (CB, LB, CDM, etc.).
*   **Tactical Events:** News pop-ups, such as the "10-0-0 Formation" item, suggest that tactical decisions are handled through dialogue choices ("Be creative" vs. "Doesn't make sense") rather than a dedicated tactical board.

### **6. Awards and Special Events**
The UI frequently interrupts the flow with celebratory or situational screens:
*   **Awards:** High-impact pop-ups for "MVP of the game," "World Footballer" candidate selections, and the "Award Ceremony" for World Player of the Year.
*   **Random Events:** "Culture Study" (choosing between Science, Arts, or History) and "Windfall" (finding money) provide attribute or fan boosts.
*   **Injury/Recovery:** An "Injury Treatment" screen offers choices like "Basic Treat" or "Expert Surgery," while a "Rest at home" screen manages energy recovery.

### **7. Observed UI Limitations**
*   **Versus Mode:** No dedicated Versus or real-time multiplayer UI was observed; the walkthrough focused entirely on the single-player career simulation.
*   **Direct Control:** The UX indicates a management-style simulation; there is no interface for direct on-pitch control of the player during matches.
