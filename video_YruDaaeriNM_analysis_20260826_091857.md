The following is a technical analysis of the managerial and career simulation systems shown in the *Football Rising Star* gameplay, focusing on the UI elements and progression mechanics.

### **1. Mode Selection and Initial Configuration**
*   **Mode Selection (00:08 - 00:20):** The main menu presents two primary gameplay paths: **[Player Mode]** and **[Coach Mode]**. The user selects **[Player Mode]**, which functions as a single-player career management simulation.
*   **Role Creation (00:21 - 01:00):** 
    *   **Birthplace:** Options include China, Argentina, Brazil, England, Spain, Italy, Germany, France, Malaysia, and Others.
    *   **Position Selection:** The UI allows for specific role assignment: **[GK]** (Goalkeeper), **[CB]**, **[LB]**, **[RB]**, **[CDM]**, **[CM]**, **[CAM]**, **[LM]**, **[RM]**, **[ST]**.
    *   **Avatar Selection:** Multiple stylized character portraits are available.

### **2. Club Management and Career Progression**
*   **Contract Signing (01:21):** A **[Signed successfully]** popup confirms the player's entry into a club (e.g., **US Boulogne**).
*   **Main Management Hub (01:37):** The dashboard displays:
    *   **Player Stats:** Rating (e.g., 66), Age, Value.
    *   **Currencies:** **[Money]** and **[Diamonds]**.
    *   **Navigation:** Buttons for **[Home]**, **[Ability]**, **[Training]**, **[Personal]**, and **[Club]**.
*   **League Progression (03:14 - 03:26):** 
    *   **Match Rounds:** Displays results for specific leagues (e.g., **[Round 1 of French B]**).
    *   **League Tables:** A comprehensive table showing Rank, Team, Played, Won, Draw, Loss, Goal Difference (GD), and Points.

### **3. Technical Management Systems**
*   **Attribute Management (01:58, 06:01):** The **[Assign EXP]** screen allows for manual distribution of experience points into specific technical attributes:
    *   **Goalkeeper Specifics:** Strike, Reaction, Hand Toss, GK Kick, Save Penalty, Hold Position, GK Pass Ball, Command Defense.
    *   **Physicals:** Balance, Jump, Willpower.
*   **Training System (04:29 - 05:30):**
    *   **Regular Training:** Categories include **[Short]**, **[Dribbling]**, **[Speed]**, **[Stamina]**, **[Pass]**, **[Defense]**, and **[Physical]**.
    *   **Trick Training:** Unlocks specific skills based on attribute thresholds (e.g., "Unlock at Willpower level 10").
    *   **Personal Training:** A hiring system for specialized coaches categorized as **[Junior]**, **[Senior]**, and **[Master]**, affecting EXP recovery and training effects.
*   **Transfer and Contract Handling (05:33, 09:40):**
    *   **Personal Finance:** Tracks weekly income and spending.
    *   **Negotiation:** Options for **[Ask for pay]**, **[Transfer]**, and **[Negotiation]**.
    *   **Transfer Offers:** External clubs provide contract offers showing their league ranking, the competitiveness of the position (e.g., **[Slightly competitive]**), and a proposed weekly salary.

### **4. Match Simulation and Awards**
*   **Match Simulation (01:46, 05:54):** Matches are simulated with a list of participating players and their performance ratings (e.g., 7.2). A live scoreboard and audience count are visible.
*   **Hall of Honor (05:51):** Tracks long-term managerial/career achievements like **[World Player of the Year]**, **[European Golden Shoe]**, and **[League MVP]**.
*   **Award Ceremonies (12:54 - 13:22):** Automated selection screens for **[World Footballer]** and **[Player of the Year]** based on seasonal performance.

### **5. Seasonal Events**
*   **Winter Break (12:43):** A transition period where matches cease, and the player can focus on training or endorsements.
*   **Endorsements (12:50):** A system to increase fame and currency through weekly endorsements.
